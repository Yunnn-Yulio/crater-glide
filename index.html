Crater Glide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

Crater Glide does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress (crystals, upgrades, best distance) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This processing is done by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy" button in the main menu.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

Crater Glide is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
